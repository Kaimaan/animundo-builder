# Choose the home

## Job

Choose how a game home for the $HABITAT looks, where the $ANIMAL lives near $LAT, $LON. Code makes the
home from your choices; it keeps the land above the water and sets every number. You choose words and
colours only.

## Input

What the research found around it:

$SURROUNDINGS

What the $ANIMAL eats: $FOODS.

The closest existing home, the starting point (its colours are a northern forest's; change them to this
habitat's):

$BASE

Plants the game already has: $PLANTS. Pick one only if it truly grows here; otherwise none. Tree shapes the
game draws: spruce (dark cone), pine (tall bare trunk, crown on top), birch (white trunk, small crown),
round (broadleaf tree), rowan (small tree). Floating plants (water lilies, water lettuce) are water_life, not
plants.

## Hard rules

- Real for this habitat: colours as a field guide's picture of the place shows them; trees as its main trees
  look, in the closest shape; foods that really grow or live here.
- The main water follows the research: a river home has water "river", not "lake".
- No real place names, no people.
