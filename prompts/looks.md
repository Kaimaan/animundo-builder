# A short looks call

## Job

One short call about $NAME$LATIN_HINT, $CONTEXT. Three searches
at most. Answer with JSON only, no prose.

## Wanted

- `latin`: its binomial, like "Lepus timidus".
- `size_cm`: body length for an animal, height for a plant.
- `shoulder_cm` (or `height_cm` for birds and fish) and
  `tail_cm`, each with its `source`; null when it has none.
- `colours`: its main colours in plain words.
- `marks`: 2 to 3 marks or shape notes, like "white tail" or
  "needle leaves".
- `year`: for a plant, its leaves, flowers and fruit through
  the year, one or two sentences; null for an animal.
- `sources`: the pages the facts came from, most important
  first.

## Searched, not remembered

Every fact comes from a search you ran in this call: the
latin name, the size, the colours, the marks. A fact without
a search stays out.
